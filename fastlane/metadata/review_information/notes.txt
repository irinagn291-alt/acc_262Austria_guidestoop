The only usage description in the build settings is for the camera and reads: This app does not use the camera. The app never asks for camera access, so no system permission alert appears. There is no login, no in-app purchase, no user-generated content, no account deletion flow and no App Tracking Transparency prompt. Guidestoop is a personal waymap for daily chores. The next camp is a road the user picks. It is for people who will plant one stake a day if they then drag the walker onto a cairn they choose. No credentials or demo account are needed. After a cold launch the first cover is onboarding. The first page is titled Stake the cairn and says Stand on the occupied camp. One chore a day plants a stake. A second stake the same day is refused. Tap Next. The second page is titled Hold the streak and says Yesterday must hold a stake or the streak drops. Three days on this camp opens the gate. Tap Next. The third page is titled Drag one road and says The gate sprouts two forward camps. A further stake does not pick. Drag the walker onto one cairn. Tap Next. The fourth page is titled The sibling seals and says That drag writes the chosen edge and seals the other for this map. Five stakes in a week open the ridge. Tap Open the waymap. Skip on any page also opens the waymap. If onboarding does not appear, the waymap is already open. If the waymap is empty it reads The path is closed. Finish three days to open the next node. Tap Stake today's cairn. The header then shows days, level and the camp name, starting at Gate Stoop, with crumbs labeled Journal, Hero and Settings. Tap Journal. When no road is written it reads No road is written yet. Stake three days on this camp, then drag the walker onto one cairn. Use Back to the waymap or Close. Tap Hero. After a stake it shows level, XP and days here, plus 50 bonus XP when five cairns stand this week. Use Close. Tap Settings. After a stake it says The waymap stays on this device. One stake a day. A drag writes one road. It lists Stakes and Chosen roads. The controls are Contact, Re-run onboarding and Reset all data. Reset asks Erase every stake and sealed road? with Reset all data and Cancel. Use Close. To open two roads, complete Stake today's cairn on three consecutive days on the occupied camp. The header becomes Take one road. From Gate Stoop the line reads Drag the walker onto Moor Knoll or Peat Ford. The other road seals. From Moor Knoll the roads are Granite Stack and Lichen Rise. From Peat Ford they are Mist Hollow and Iron Gall. Drag the walker onto one cairn, or tap Take this road, or tap Take Moor Knoll or Take Peat Ford. If the header already reads Take one road, use those same controls. After five Stake today's cairn actions in the same week a Ridge overlay appears and says Five stakes opened the week. Claim bonus XP. Tap Claim the ridge. Stakes, chosen roads, XP and the waymap stay on this device. Contact in Settings opens the support page. Behaviour does not differ by region. No licence or regulated-industry paperwork applies because this is a personal daily-stake waymap with no clinical advice and no paid service.

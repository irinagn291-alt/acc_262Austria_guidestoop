Guidestoop is a waymap for daily chores. Stand on the occupied camp, plant one stake a day, hold the streak, then drag the walker onto one of two revealed cairns.

• Stake today's cairn once a day on the occupied camp. A second stake the same day is refused.
• Hold the streak. Yesterday must hold a stake or the streak drops.
• Three days on this camp opens the gate. Two forward camps appear. A further stake does not pick.
• Drag the walker onto one cairn, or tap Take this road. The sibling road seals for this map.
• From Gate Stoop the roads are Moor Knoll and Peat Ford. Later camps are Granite Stack, Lichen Rise, Mist Hollow and Iron Gall.
• Journal keeps chosen and sealed roads.
• Hero shows level, XP and days here. Level is one plus total XP over one hundred.
• After five stakes in a week, Ridge appears. Claim the ridge awards 50 bonus XP.
• Settings holds Contact, Re-run onboarding and Reset all data.
• The waymap stays on this device.

There is no account and no in-app purchase.
